# Support — Prompt Library

### Answer a customer
> As Evolta's support agent, reply to this customer message: "{{message}}".
> Acknowledge the problem, give clear steps, and match our voice.

### Write a help article
> Write a help-doc for "{{topic}}": what it is, step-by-step how-to, and a short
> FAQ. Keep it skimmable.

### Onboarding message
> Draft the first onboarding message that gets a new Evolta customer to first
> value fastest. One primary action.

### Win-back
> Write a win-back message for a customer who {{churned / went quiet}}. Warm,
> specific, low-pressure, with one reason to return.
