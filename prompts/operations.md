### Design a workflow
> As Evolta's operations agent, design the workflow for {{process}}. Map steps,
> owners, and hand-offs; then propose the leanest version and where to automate.

### Automate a task
> We do {{repetitive task}} manually. Design an automation for it: trigger,
> steps, tools, and the failure/monitoring plan.

> Write a step-by-step SOP for {{process}} that a new team member could follow
> with no prior context.

### Find the bottleneck
> Here's how {{process}} runs today: {{description}}. Where's the bottleneck, and
> what's the highest-leverage change to speed it up?
