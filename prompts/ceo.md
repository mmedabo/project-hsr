# CEO — Prompt Library

### Set quarterly goals
> As Evolta's CEO, using `company/PROFILE.md`, set the top 3 goals for this
> quarter. Rank them, give each a measurable outcome and owning department, and
> update `company/ROADMAP.md`.

### Prioritize a backlog
> As CEO, here are the things we could do: {{list}}. Rank them by impact vs.
> effort for Evolta right now, recommend the top one, and write the brief for
> the department that owns it.

### Make a decision
> As CEO, decide: {{decision}}. Give options, the trade-offs against our goals
> and constraints, your recommendation, and log it in the decisions table.

### Weekly review
> As CEO, review progress on `company/ROADMAP.md`. What's on track, what's
> blocked, and what should each department focus on this week?
