## Current focus
- **North-star goal:** Convert the Blue Cloud Softech distribution opportunity
  into a **signed, executable partnership** — a completed business analysis of
  their demos and a founder-ready term sheet — while keeping Evolta's two
  credibility/innovation assets moving at low cost: Agent Firewall (maintain as
  a live proof point) and Form Coach (advance to a grant-ready MVP). Concrete
  target: **term sheet on the table with Blue Cloud by end of quarter**, Form
  Coach at a demoable grant milestone, Pianio unblocked into a repo.
- **Quarter:** Q4 2026
- **Focus decision (supersedes 2026-08-03 single-product decision):** Run **4
  tracks in parallel** with distribution as the MAJOR focus. Distribution leads
  because it leverages **Blue Cloud's build, not Evolta's engineering time** —
  it is a commercial/analysis motion, not a build motion. Agent Firewall and
  Form Coach are **low-cost parallel** credibility/innovation tracks. Pianio is
  a **contained unblock task** (get into a repo) before any further investment.
  The one guarded risk: engineering bandwidth over-extension — only ONE build
  track (Form Coach) may consume real engineering time this quarter.
